What's broken, and for how long

Red bars are jobs that stopped after an error, or can no longer run. Gray bars were turned off by someone. Nothing alerted the team in any of these cases.

Everything runs on its own clock

A typical day in Eastern time. No step waits for the one before it, so a slow load upstream means reports and Salesforce updates run on stale data without anyone knowing. Coalesce is the busiest clock: about 156 live refreshes a day, plus copies running in QA and DEV.

Hover or tap a mark for details. Fivetran sync times for MLB data aren't confirmed, so they're left off. Times converted from UTC at Eastern Standard Time. Coalesce times come from its Jobs Schedule page.

Where the work happens

Snowflake is split into a test area (DEV), QA, and the live area (PROD). The original 2025 design says live work belongs in PROD. In practice, a lot of it runs in DEV, with the most powerful role.

Inside Salesforce

Salesforce runs its own automations every time a record is saved. Updates from Snowflake set off the ones on Account every hour, and possibly the ones on Contact, Opportunity, and Lead.
